Audit the codebase for structural and architectural quality with the goal of refactoring it into a more professional, maintainable codebase. Focus on separation of concerns, modularity, clear boundaries between layers, configuration management, and alignment with principles commonly seen in well-structured production systems, including Twelve-Factor App concepts where applicable. Look for god classes, oversized files, mixed responsibilities, tightly coupled modules, business logic leaking into controllers or UI layers, poor environment/config handling, duplicated logic, weak abstractions, and unclear ownership of responsibilities. Ignore code style, naming, and formatting unless they directly contribute to architectural problems. For each issue found: describe the structural problem, estimate the severity (high/medium/low), explain why it hurts maintainability or scalability, propose the refactor, then implement it. Start with the highest-impact issues first.
