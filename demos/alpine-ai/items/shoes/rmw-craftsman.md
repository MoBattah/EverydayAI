# Notes

Premium Australian Chelsea boot from R.M.Williams. The Comfort Craftsman offers refined construction suitable for business casual settings. The slip-on design with elastic gussets provides convenience while maintaining a polished appearance. Versatile enough for both casual and professional environments.

Handcrafted from a single piece of yearling leather in our Adelaide workshop, the Comfort Craftsman is a versatile shoe for light work, daily dressing or more formal wear. This boot has a rubber welt sole, classic square chisel toe and a comfort innersole.
